# Study Better

Study Better is an academic productivity application designed to help students organise their studies and manage their academic responsibilities.

Whether you are keeping track of assignments, organising modules or monitoring your study activities, Study Better brings useful academic tools together in one application.

## Features

**Module Management**
Create and manage your academic modules in one place.

**Assignment Management**
Record assignments, monitor their progress and keep track of upcoming deadlines.

**Study Sessions**
Use the study timer to manage your study sessions and record the time spent studying.

**Academic Progress Analytics**
Monitor your study activity, completed assignments, XP, levels and module progress.

**Academic Resources**
Save and organise useful learning resources, including notes, study guides, past papers and external resource links.

**Achievements**
Track academic milestones and unlock achievements as you make progress.

**Notifications and Reminders**
Receive notifications and reminders to help you stay aware of your academic activities.

**Offline Support**
Access locally saved academic information when an internet connection is unavailable. Changes that require server synchronisation may be updated when connectivity is restored.

Study Better aims to make academic organisation simpler and help students build better study habits.
