# Study Better Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 5 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Study Better is an academic productivity application designed to help students manage modules, assignments, study sessions, academic resources and progress.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Handle

Study Better may handle the following information:

- **Account information:** Name, email address and account identifier used during registration or Google sign-in.
- **Academic information:** Modules, assignments, study sessions and academic resources created by users.
- **Application information:** Device notification tokens and notification preferences required for notification functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

- Create and manage user accounts.
- Provide access to the application's academic management features.
- Save and synchronise academic information.
- Calculate academic progress and achievements.
- Deliver relevant notifications and reminders.

## 4. Data Storage

Study Better uses local device storage to retain certain information and support offline access.

Account and academic information may also be stored on the application's backend database to support online functionality and synchronisation.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Study Better uses third-party services, including:

- **Google Sign-In:** For user authentication.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** For push notification functionality.
- **Microsoft Azure:** For backend hosting and database services.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionalities.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain stored while an account is active or while it is required to provide application functionality.

Users who wish to request account or data deletion can contact the developer using the contact information below.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Study Better is intended for students and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or information-handling practices change.
